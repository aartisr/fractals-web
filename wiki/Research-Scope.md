# Research standards and scope

**[Open Fractals Web](https://fractals.ai-aarti.com/)**

Fractals Web supports exploration, teaching, and hypothesis-generating research. Its outputs should be discussed with the method, parameters, source image, and limitations in view.

## Tumor-complexity workflow

The workflow separates AI localization from fractal-morphology measurement. It tests an important research question: whether a standardized complexity feature could add information beyond AI localization for a pre-specified task.

The current product does not diagnose disease, predict outcomes, recommend treatment, or claim clinical validation. Those uses would require a locked protocol, expert reference standards, comparison against AI-only baselines, independent external cohorts, and appropriate clinical oversight.
