Fractals Web: visual science workbench

Fractals Web is a free, browser-based space for exploring patterns across scale, measuring image complexity, comparing visual evidence, and keeping the context behind a result.

Start by generating a fractal, then use a focused region of interest to investigate how a pattern occupies space. Box-counting results include visible scale data and fit-quality context so an estimate can be interpreted with its method rather than treated as a standalone verdict.

Educators and learners can use guided challenges to practice observation, explanation, and reproducible documentation. The biomedical demonstration is an educational research prototype: it presents fractal morphology as a candidate feature to study alongside AI localization and is not a diagnostic, prognostic, or treatment tool.
